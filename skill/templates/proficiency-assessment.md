## Interpretation rules

Do not infer proficiency from time spent, topic count, self-reported familiarity, or one polished artifact alone. Prefer multiple observations across comparable and novel tasks. Keep claims proportional to the evidence and distinguish syllabus completion from professional authority, licensure, or field-level expertise.
