# Domain safety profiles

Use a safety profile when a portfolio includes medicine or clinical learning, human-subject research, robotics or physical hardware, cybersecurity, finance, aviation, laboratory work, or regulated engineering.

A profile records permitted self-study, simulation boundaries, supervision requirements, authorization requirements, prohibited shortcuts, evidence that cannot be self-certified, escalation conditions, and current-version or regional verification needs. Keep the profile attached to affected nodes and propagate its restrictions to projects, bridges, tools, data, and evidence claims.

Use synthetic or approved data, simulation, sandboxing, supervision, protective equipment, institutional review, and documented authorization where appropriate. Do not infer professional competence, licensure, clinical authority, legal permission, financial suitability, or safety clearance from a learning plan or self-reported completion.
