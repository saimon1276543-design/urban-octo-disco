# Portfolio Health and Audits

Use this reference to audit a learning portfolio without reducing it to one unsupported score.

## Signals

Check dangling prerequisites, goals without outcomes, capabilities without evidence, excessive active branches, duplicate foundations, overloaded/decorative bridges, stale references, update debt, oversized projects, missing maintenance, missing safety/authorization, unrealistic compute/data/infrastructure assumptions, unsupported current claims, weak provenance/retention, and missing recovery/observability/deletion behavior.

## Finding contract

Each finding reports severity, affected stable IDs, signal, likely cause, confidence, reversible repair proposal, evidence needed, and next review trigger. Classify as blocker, high, medium, low, or informational. Keep unknown distinct from healthy.

## Repair policy

Prefer the smallest reversible repair: split a project, defer a branch, add a prerequisite, replace a duplicate, attach evidence, queue revalidation, reduce concurrency, add a safety boundary, or define a recovery test. Do not automatically rewrite goals, erase evidence, or commit material route changes without preserving a proposal and rationale.
