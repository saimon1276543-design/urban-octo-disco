# Durable Domain Catalog

Use this reference when a request spans the scientific, medical, computing, or intelligence families below. Load narrower references when available and preserve each domain's irreducible core.

## Robotics

Build from mathematics, mechanics, electronics, embedded systems, sensing, estimation, control, kinematics, dynamics, perception, planning, manipulation, mobile robotics, simulation, integration, testing, and safety. Use spline interpolation and Kalman filtering only with explicit assumptions, model limits, and evidence.

## iBCI and electrochemistry/materials

Keep neuroanatomy, neurophysiology, neural signals, acquisition, analog front ends, signal processing, decoding, ML, embedded systems, human factors, ethics, clinical translation, and validation distinct from electrochemistry/materials. The materials route includes thermodynamics, kinetics, redox, electrode/electrolyte/tissue interfaces, charge transfer, polarization, impedance, corrosion, metals/alloys, coatings, surface science, biocompatibility, and failure analysis. A software or simulation artifact does not establish clinical competence or implant safety.

## Medical education and examinations

Use an AIIMS Delhi-aligned target architecture across biomedical sciences, organ systems, pathology, pharmacology, microbiology, forensic and community medicine, clinical medicine, surgery, allied specialties, diagnostics, therapeutics, communication, professionalism, public health, evidence-based medicine, and integrated cases. Treat official curriculum, INI-CET, and NEET-PG rules, eligibility, dates, formats, and notices as current-source overlays. Do not present an informal route as an official syllabus or clinical authorization.

## ML and AI

Build from linear algebra, probability, statistics, optimization, algorithms, regression, classification, confusion matrices, cross-validation, bias/variance, overfitting/underfitting, unsupervised/supervised/semi-supervised learning, neural networks, feedforward/backpropagation, attention, embeddings, tokens, weights, parameters, GNNs, deep learning, generative models, SFT, RLHF, distillation, mixture-of-experts, evaluation, robustness, and responsible deployment. Train-from-scratch goals require realistic data, compute, memory, systems, and evaluation modeling.

## Programming and systems

Share computing foundations, algorithms, data structures, testing, debugging, version control, and software design. Preserve distinct tracks for Python, C++, Rust, Go, R, JavaScript, Shell/Bash, and Vim. Route Linux administration, networking, permissions, processes, services, storage, logs, backup, automation, and safe operations through the systems references.

## OSINT

Teach question framing, collection requirements, source discovery, provenance, corroboration, public records, archives, documents, images, geospatial reasoning, entity resolution, timelines, link analysis, hypothesis testing, uncertainty, reporting, and reproducibility. Maintain lawful access, privacy, anti-doxxing, non-escalation, and source-safety boundaries.

## Evidence rule

Every route needs a capability outcome, prerequisites, observable practice, evidence artifact, and boundary. Topic exposure, a toy demo, or a bridge project cannot silently prove independent or regulated competence.
