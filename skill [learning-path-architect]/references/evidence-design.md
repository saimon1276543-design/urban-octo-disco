# Evidence Design

Use this reference whenever the learner asks how to know whether learning is complete, requests a proficiency target, or is using progress data to unlock new branches.

## Evidence ladder

Choose the smallest evidence that matches the target:

| Evidence level | What it shows | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Explain/recall | Can describe or retrieve the idea | Foundational understanding |
| Guided performance | Can perform with a worked example or checklist | Early practice |
| Familiar independent performance | Can perform without step-by-step help in a known context | Competent routine use |
| Bounded transfer | Can apply to a new but comparable problem | Reliable capability |
| Ambiguous judgment | Can choose, debug, compare, and explain trade-offs | Proficient work |
| Sustained real-world artifact | Can produce and maintain an outcome over time | Advanced or portfolio evidence |

Do not require the highest level for every objective. Match evidence to the intended proficiency and performance context.

## False positives

Reduce confidence when evidence is only tutorial completion, copied output, recognition without recall, an answer produced with hidden assistance, a familiar example repeated verbatim, speed without transfer, or an artifact that cannot be explained or debugged.

## Evidence record

For each major milestone record the objective, performance context, support allowed, artifact or result, evidence source, date/freshness, reviewer or verification method, confidence, and the smallest gap exposed if the criterion failed.

## Progress decision

Advance when the evidence meets the stated context. Remediate when the smallest gap is identifiable. Maintain when a capability is complete but needs retrieval or reuse. Do not increase capacity or concurrency based on completion speed when retention or transfer evidence is weak.

## Proportionality

Use one small evidence task for a focused map. Add multiple contexts, transfer, retention, or sustained artifacts only when the requested level, risk, or long horizon justifies them.
