# Source Health and Conflicts

Use this reference when current sources disagree, age, become inaccessible, or differ by region, version, or use case.

## Health states

Use qualitative states: current, aging, stale, inaccessible, contradicted, or context-limited. Freshness is separate from learner mastery and must not be converted into a false numerical certainty.

## Conflict handling

Preserve competing claims temporarily. Record authority, jurisdiction, version, date, context, and affected nodes. Prefer the strongest source applicable to the learner’s context, but keep the alternative and rationale visible. Escalate clinical, regulatory, safety, security, and human-subjects conflicts. If the conflict changes the route and cannot be resolved, mark the route decision unresolved and ask only the focused question needed.

Do not promote a new paper, tool, framework, or industry claim to mandatory scope merely because it is recent. First establish relevance to the learner’s north star, dependency graph, target context, evidence standard, and durability.
