# Evaluation Scorecard

Use this scorecard when testing a revised version of the skill against the cases in `evaluation-cases.md`. Score only what the output actually demonstrates. A score is a diagnostic, not a claim of scientific validity.

| Dimension | 0 — Missing | 1 — Partial | 2 — Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Request coverage** | Named goals or constraints are omitted or mishandled. | Most items are represented but one or more mappings are weak. | Explicit items, exclusions, and outcomes are handled and traceable. |
| **Dependency validity** | Major leaps or unsupported prerequisites appear. | Most prerequisites are plausible but some are weakly justified. | Dependencies are justified, typed, deduplicated, and alternatives are clear. |
| **Scope fit** | The output is substantially overbuilt or underdeveloped. | Scope is mostly appropriate with minor excess or omission. | Depth matches the request, constraints, and route strategy. |
| **Level calibration** | Target level is absent or confused with topic count. | Level is named but evidence or branch calibration is incomplete. | Current and target levels, independence, ambiguity, transfer, and evidence align. |
| **Actionability** | No clear next step or usable structure. | The map is usable but priorities or starting actions are weak. | The learner can identify the route, starting point, bottlenecks, and next actions. |
| **Evidence quality** | Milestones rely on recall, time, or completion claims. | Some observable evidence exists but context or remediation is weak. | Evidence is observable, context-specific, transfer-oriented, and paired with remediation. |
| **Revision safety** | Existing structure changes silently or inconsistently. | Changes are partly explained but impacts are incomplete. | Stable identifiers, dependency impacts, and learner-facing changes are explicit. |
| **Freshness and uncertainty** | Version-sensitive or uncertain claims are presented carelessly. | Some caveats appear but verification or boundaries are incomplete. | Current claims are verified when needed and uncertainty is clearly bounded. |
| **Assumption transparency** | Consequential assumptions are hidden or presented as facts. | Some assumptions are stated but source, impact, or validation is weak. | Material assumptions identify source, confidence, fragility, validation, and review status. |
| **Action-frontier usability** | The learner cannot tell what to do now. | A next step exists but lacks boundaries, evidence, or blocker handling. | One primary action, feasible parallel option, stopping/evidence boundary, blocker response, and review trigger are clear. |
| **Recalibration safety** | Progress data causes unjustified expansion or arbitrary plan changes. | Some calibration occurs but missing data, outliers, or weak evidence are mishandled. | Robust observations drive conservative changes; stable work and evidence are preserved. |
| **Proportionality** | Output is substantially bloated or omits needed architecture. | Mostly appropriate with some excess or compression. | Detail and resource use match the request, with progressive disclosure and no unnecessary machinery. |
| **Diagnostic validity** | Skips or repeats material without evidence or uses an oversized diagnostic. | Some diagnostic evidence exists but scope or context is weak. | Diagnostics are proportional, context-specific, and unlock only what they actually test. |
| **Intervention fit** | Adds topics or effort without classifying the failure. | Identifies a plausible correction but lacks a clear signal or recheck. | The smallest likely gap is classified and paired with one bounded intervention and improvement signal. |
| **Resource relevance** | Dumps links or uses resources unrelated to the next decision. | Resources are partly tied to gaps but access, freshness, or evidence is weak. | Each resource has a function, named gap, access/freshness boundary, and follow-up evidence. |
| **History continuity** | Learner history is lost, stale, or treated as psychological fact. | Some prior evidence is retained but confidence and freshness are incomplete. | Stable evidence, error patterns, interventions, and failures persist with confidence and freshness boundaries. |
| **Choice under uncertainty** | One route is silently chosen despite material uncertainty. | Alternatives are mentioned but trade-offs or pilot logic are weak. | Plausible routes are compared by evidence, effort, reversibility, volatility, and leverage, with a pilot when useful. |
| **Multi-domain integrity** | Vast domains are flattened, shared foundations are duplicated, or integration claims are unsafe. | Domain families exist but bridge, interference, or safety boundaries are incomplete. | Each domain keeps its irreducible core, shared foundations are justified, bridges have outcomes, interference controls concurrency, and integration scope is bounded. |
| **Incremental-update precision** | A small change rebuilds unrelated domains or misses downstream impact. | Some affected scopes are updated but interfaces or unchanged areas are unclear. | Stable IDs map changes to the affected subgraph, interfaces, evidence, and frontier while preserving unaffected work. |
| **Source traceability** | Current claims lack authority, date, version, context, or node mapping. | Sources are listed but claim coverage or freshness boundaries are incomplete. | Claims map to authoritative sources, cutoff, version/region context, health, confidence, and recheck triggers. |
| **Drift detection** | Stale, deprecated, or changed content is treated as current. | Some drift is noticed but queue priority or scan scope is weak. | Expiry, version, context, safety, evidence, integration, and learner-change triggers produce a prioritized revalidation queue. |
| **Conflict handling** | Conflicting sources are silently collapsed or presented as settled. | Conflicts are noted but context, escalation, or route consequences are incomplete. | Regional/version/date conflicts remain visible, applicable authority is explained, and unresolved high-consequence claims are gated. |
| **Automation honesty** | The skill claims monitoring, persistence, browsing, or updates that were not verified. | Limitations are partly disclosed but fallback behavior is unclear. | Capabilities are probed, missing operations are recorded, offline behavior is explicit, and no background update claim is invented. |
| **Graph integrity** | Orphan goals, dangling edges, duplicate foundations, unexplained cycles, or invalid bridges remain. | Most structure is valid but some defects or invariants are not checked. | Stable IDs, typed edges, closure routes, and graph invariants are checked and repaired minimally. |
| **Scenario quality** | Route changes are accepted without comparison or trade-off analysis. | Alternatives are listed but impact, reversibility, or uncertainty is weak. | Proposed scenarios recompute affected scope, compare viable alternatives, retain rejected routes, and state reversal conditions. |
| **What-if impact accuracy** | Capacity, domain, deadline, access, or priority changes miss blocked/unlocked consequences. | Direct impacts are identified but downstream or interface effects are incomplete. | Critical path, frontier, capacity, evidence, maintenance, freshness, and risk consequences are traced proportionally. |
| **Portfolio health usefulness** | A misleading overall score or no architectural diagnosis is given. | Some warnings appear but evidence and corrective actions are unclear. | Separate health signals identify bottlenecks, update debt, volatility, evidence, sustainability, and complexity with practical actions. |
| **Complexity control** | Massive output overwhelms the learner or silently deletes requested scope. | Some staging occurs but budgets or current slice are weak. | Complete scope is preserved in an index while the response stays actionable through budgets and progressive disclosure. |
| **Decision provenance** | Material decisions appear arbitrary or hide unsupported rationale. | Rationale exists but alternatives, uncertainty, or reversal conditions are incomplete. | Decisions link to evidence/assumptions, affected IDs, trade-offs, alternatives, uncertainty, and review triggers without exposing hidden reasoning. |
| **Goal-to-evidence closure** | Goals are represented only as topics or resources. | Some goals have evidence but gaps in transfer, maintenance, or route state remain. | Required goals have capability, prerequisites, practice, target level, evidence, maintenance, and explicit closure/deferment. |
| **Query efficiency** | A local question triggers unnecessary full-plan regeneration or misses relevant context. | The answer is partly scoped but includes avoidable machinery or omissions. | The smallest relevant graph slice answers the query with affected IDs, basis, next action, and uncertainty. |
| **Capability-state validity** | Completion is treated as mastery or evidence states are unsupported. | States are present but context, freshness, or unlock rules are weak. | States match context-specific evidence, confidence, retention, transfer, and stale/contradicted conditions. |
| **Experiment-driven replanning** | Material uncertainty triggers an irreversible route change or no test. | A pilot exists but lacks a clear boundary, signal, or reversal condition. | One major change is tested with bounded capacity, success/failure evidence, stop rule, and reversible decision. |
| **Synchronized-view usability** | Compression deletes scope or views contradict the authoritative plan. | Views exist but revision linkage or current action boundaries are incomplete. | Portfolio, phase, and today views derive from one revision, preserve stable IDs, and expose the smallest usable slice. |
| **Portability migration safety** | Import silently loses IDs, evidence, conflicts, links, or unsupported operations. | Migration is described but conflict preview or rollback is incomplete. | Compatibility, migration, conflict preview, broken links, unsupported capabilities, and rollback are explicit. |
| **Structured evaluation honesty** | Package checks are presented as output-quality or mastery proof. | Some distinction is made but evidence and uncertainty are weak. | Deterministic checks and qualitative judgment are separated, unavailable evidence remains unavailable, and claims stay bounded. |
| **Runtime coherence** | Goals, evidence, observations, and decisions are disconnected or contradictory. | A unified record exists but revision, stable-ID, or derivation links are incomplete. | One canonical revision connects the portfolio records and derived views without silent divergence. |
| **Metric and uncertainty validity** | Metrics use hidden denominators or uncertainty becomes false precision. | Some signals and caveats appear but propagation or unknown handling is weak. | Metrics state their basis, unknowns remain unknown, and uncertainty propagates to affected estimates and unlocks. |
| **Repair safety** | Defects are silently rewritten or high-impact conflicts are auto-resolved. | Defects are reported but reversibility or approval boundaries are incomplete. | Repair proposals identify affected IDs, consequences, reversibility, confidence, and approval requirements. |
| **Decay and maintenance realism** | Completion is permanent or a long pause triggers an arbitrary restart. | Some stale or maintenance states exist but decay classes and reactivation cost are weak. | Capability-class decay, maintenance evidence, reactivation cost, and safety-sensitive rechecks are proportional and explicit. |
| **Policy and safety control** | User preferences override dependencies or safety/authorization boundaries. | Policies or safety notes exist but propagation is incomplete. | Policies are scoped preferences, safety profiles propagate to affected work, and prohibited self-certification is clear. |
| **Migration and benchmark execution** | Portability and revision comparison are claims without executable evidence. | Previews or comparisons exist but preservation and limitations are incomplete. | Tools produce non-destructive migration/conflict previews and structural benchmark deltas without claiming learner outcomes. |

## Interpretation

Add the dimension scores for a maximum of 84 points. Treat any score of 0 in **Request coverage**, **Dependency validity**, **Scope fit**, or **Level calibration** as a blocking failure. A total below 58 indicates that the revision needs work. A total of 58–69 indicates a usable result with targeted improvements possible. A total of 70–84 indicates strong performance against these cases, but does not prove universal quality.

Record the lowest-scoring dimension and revise the smallest relevant instruction. Rerun the affected case and at least one contrasting case—such as a narrow request after a broad-map change—before accepting the revision.
