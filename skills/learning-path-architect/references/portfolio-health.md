# Portfolio Health

Use this reference to diagnose the architecture, not to judge the learner. Report separate signals rather than one overall score.

| Signal | Inspect | Warning | Possible action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coverage completeness | Goal-to-node and goal-to-evidence mapping | Required goal has no closure route | Add, clarify, defer, or mark limitation |
| Prerequisite integrity | Dangling edges, cycles, unsupported leaps | Unreachable or circular required work | Repair the smallest graph defect |
| Frontier clarity | Current actions and boundaries | Too many active actions or none actionable | Compress and expose one primary action |
| Bottleneck concentration | Nodes gating many families | One fragile bottleneck blocks portfolio | Pilot, strengthen, or create alternative |
| Bridge leverage | Domains served per bridge and evidence | Bridge is decorative or overloaded | Bound, split, or retire bridge |
| Volatility exposure | Volatile nodes and source health | Active route depends on stale claims | Revalidate or use durable alternative |
| Evidence sufficiency | Milestone evidence and transfer | Topics mapped without performance proof | Add smallest closure task |
| Branch sustainability | Observed capacity and switching | Planned branches are not sustained | Reduce concurrency or maintenance load |
| Update debt | Queued stale scopes and conflicts | Critical revalidation remains unresolved | Prioritize or gate affected route |
| Learner-facing complexity | Detail, choices, tracker burden | Learner cannot tell what to do now | Stage, summarize, or defer detail |

Use evidence from the graph, tracker, source ledger, update runs, and learner context. A warning is a decision prompt, not a diagnosis of motivation or ability.
