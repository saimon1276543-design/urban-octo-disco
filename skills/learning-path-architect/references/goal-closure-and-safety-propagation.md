# Goal Closure and Safety Propagation

## Goal-to-evidence closure

For every required goal, verify a capability definition, prerequisite coverage, target level, practice route, evidence artifact or performance, transfer/retention condition when relevant, maintenance/revalidation rule, route state, and active/deferred explanation. Flag goals that have topics but no credible performance evidence.

A credential or professional role may require institutional education, supervised practice, licensing, authorization, or accountability outside the plan. Preserve that limitation explicitly.

## Safety propagation

Propagate a safety or authorization state through an actual data, activity, hardware, or operational dependency—not merely because two domain names are adjacent. Use ordinary, caution, supervised, restricted, regulated, or unresolved states. A software dashboard using synthetic data may not inherit clinical restrictions; a project using patient data, human subjects, hazardous hardware, or device control must inherit the relevant boundary.

For a changed risk state, identify affected nodes, evidence, projects, environments, supervision, data controls, and authorization requirements. Gate unsafe execution while allowing safe simulation, synthetic data, case analysis, or supervised practice where appropriate.
